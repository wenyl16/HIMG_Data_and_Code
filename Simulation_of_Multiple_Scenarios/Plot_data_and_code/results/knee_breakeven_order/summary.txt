Ordering of flexibility knees and economic crossover points

Economic data: Economic Breakeven Point/data/sensitivity
Only battery.xlsx rows with bat_price_factor=1.0 and hydrogen.xlsx rows with storage_factor=1.0 are used.
Flexibility data: Flexibility Turning Point/results/domestic_flexibility_knee_method_differences.csv
The saved results of the three knee methods are used without redetection; their storage-duration analysis cap is 200 h.
Economic crossover points follow the economic plotting algorithm, with penetration from 0--99.9% and an off-grid duration cap of 500 h.
The two duration caps belong to the respective threshold definitions and are retained in this comparison.

The comparison axis is renewable energy penetration, rather than elapsed or off-grid duration.
Delta p = economic crossover penetration - flexibility knee penetration; coincidence tolerance = 0.0001 percentage points.
Delta p > tolerance: the flexibility knee is ahead; Delta p < -tolerance: behind; absolute differences within tolerance: coincident.
Cases with no economic crossover or a missing flexibility knee are reported separately and excluded from the ahead/behind/coincident counts and percentage denominators.
The denominator for each method is the number of valid scenario-target combinations; a total across methods counts method-specific comparisons, not independent scenarios.
