Fig. 2 | Renewable penetration, consumption constraints and autonomy jointly determine storage-pathway economics. Renewable penetration and minimum off-grid duration for industrial (a), commercial (b), rural (c) and island (d) microgrids at renewable-consumption targets of 60%, 70%, 80%, 90% and 100%. Curve colour gives Delta LCOE (BESS minus the H2-integrated pathway): blue denotes the BESS-preferred regime and red the H2-integrated-pathway-preferred regime. Open circles denote calculated zero crossings; the solid connecting line is the economic breakeven boundary. For the island system at the 60% consumption target, Delta LCOE does not cross zero within the analysed penetration-autonomy domain; no breakeven point is reported in d and a cross at the top of the corresponding strip denotes no crossover in e. Panel e shows the signed separation between the economic breakeven penetration (p_e) and the physical turning-point penetration (p_p), Delta p = p_e - p_p. Positive values indicate that the physical transition precedes the economic crossover; negative values indicate an earlier economic crossover. Physical turning points use the maximum-curvature criterion reported with Fig. 1.
